# Brian

## As a player
Brian's decks lean toward table politics and weird interaction: forced combat and goad (Kardur, Thantis),
stealing (Kotis, Don Andres, Tergrid), chaos (Yusri coin flips, Obosh odd costs, Xantcha), meld, and
spellslinging. Brian tags commanders "Very Silly", "Hmmmm" and "Actually Good". **Fun first, winning
second.** The play log below records how the decks actually play; it beats this paragraph when they differ.

## Preferences beyond the house rules
- Plays Commander **online** (MTGO/Arena-style clients or SpellTable), so price doesn't matter and any
  card is available. Paper decks are rare; agree rules for those one at a time (Archidekt folder "Paper").
- **Universes Beyond:** avoid; ask first if a deck really needs one. The Wise Mothman deck is approved for
  UB cards, especially Fallout rad-counter cards.
- Archidekt folders: "Commander" holds the decks Brian plays, "Lists & Ideas" other people's lists and ideas,
  "Archive" retired decks.
