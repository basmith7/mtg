# Slice 12 choices

- **The player's seat comes from their Mulligan Labs player id** (`replays.account_id`: the id at the
  seats `match_seat` finds, stable across games), so the meta also covers games played with a deck that
  has no local list. Alternative: `match_seat` per game, which drops those games and could count the
  player's own commander as an opponent's.
- **Default lookback: the newest 20 completed games** (delegated). Enough to see the regulars without
  old decks dominating; `--last N` overrides.
- **Themes = `mtg.patterns` tags plus the site's own `detectedMechanics`**, each opponent deck counted
  once however many games it appears in. Without `mtg.patterns` (an older checkout) only the site's tags
  print, with a note.
- **The site's "Unknown Commander" placeholder is left out** of counts and `--names`.
- **`--names` prints most played first**, one per line.
