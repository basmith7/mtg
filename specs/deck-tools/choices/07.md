# Slice 07 choices

- **control-swap adds `o:"under the control of an opponent"`** (7 -> 9 commanders). Alternative: the spec's
  three phrases. Why: catches "enters under an opponent's control" designs like the Xantcha that Brian's
  profile names. Broader `o:"opponent gains control"` gave 19, mostly cards that steal, so it lost.
- **chaos is `o:"at random"` only** (28). Alternative: `o:random or o:"at random"` (105). Why: the extra 77
  are mostly "random" in other senses (e.g. Aang), not chaos commanders.
- **`candidates` rows carry identity**: `(name, mechanics, rank, identity)`, one more than the spec. Why:
  the output shows colours, and the commander challenge uses identity for its pool.
- **`--counts` re-sorts the top 15 by EDHREC deck count.** Alternative: rank order only. Why: Scryfall's
  `edhrec_rank` is a card's rank across all decks, not as a commander (Abby rank 15543 = 18 decks; Bill
  Ferny rank 21974 = 604).
- **Challenge brief = commander + constraint.** The art, flavour and year rolls pick a commander from
  `<pool> is:commander`; the commander roll picks one of the 10 least-played for a random mechanic,
  with pool `id<=<identity> f:commander`. The brief prints no pool size (that would take a second
  request, or paging through the whole pool); the query is printed instead.
- **Art tags**: 29 tags at 111-2240 cards each; sword (3917), tree (3195) and eye (4374) dropped as too
  loose to be a challenge, crab (87) and goat (80) as too small.
- **`--pool` searches `(<query>) (!"a" or !"b" ...)` in ~700-character chunks** (2-3 requests a deck), not
  one search of the whole pool. Why: colour/year pools run to thousands of cards (tens of pages). Basic
  lands are exempt. Lines are `!!` and exit 1 because using --pool opts into the challenge's rules.
- **`--decks DIR`** (default `decks/commander`) says whose commanders to skip, so a worktree can point
  at the main checkout read-only.
- **`--kind`** chooses challenge kinds; `challenge()` takes `exclude` so the player's own commanders
  aren't rolled.
- **Keyword map** keeps "table" out of each-player: Brian's bullet says "works for the table" and means
  control-swap.
