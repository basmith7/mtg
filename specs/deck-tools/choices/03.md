# Slice 03 choices

- **Salt from MTGJSON AtomicCards.** The probe: `edhrecSaltiness` on 83/83, 78/78 and 84/84 nonbasic cards
  (Etali, Kardur, Gonti), equal to EDHREC's card-page `salt`. Kept only as a 0.9 MB `{name: salt}` table
  in `config.CACHE/edhrec/salt.json`, rebuilt weekly; the 52 MB download is not stored. Alternative: card
  pages at 1 req/s (~85 s a deck, uncached decks only).
- **Salt and share count nonland cards only**: basics have salt scores (Forest 0.62) that would drag the
  average toward nothing.
- **`brackets.flags` returns every patterns tag**, not just MLD and extra turns, so the table line and
  the pitch read one dict; Spellbook still overrides only `mld` and `extra-turn`.
- **`patterns` no longer imports `scryfall`** (it joined faces through `scryfall.oracle_text`): pure
  modules must not pull in `http`. The two-line face join is duplicated instead.
- **`mana_value` moved from check_commander into `brackets`**, since table_feel needs the same lookup.
- **Table-touching = any tag but `extra-turn`**; `each-opponent` counts ("each opponent loses 1 life").
- **Theft also matches "You control enchanted creature"**: Treachery and Control Magic say it that way,
  never "gain control of". Donate is kept apart (`gains control` ≠ `gain control`).
- **Pitch shape** (wording delegated): up to two themes with 3+ cards ("Goad and theft deck"), else
  "Interactive deck" at 25%+ share or "Mostly self-focused deck"; then "B<n> on paper (first reason)";
  then "with …" what is present among stax / land wipes / extra-turn chains / fast combos, else "but no
  …"; the saltiest card when any is over 2.0; "it bends our house rules" when the house check fails.
  Any stax card counts as stax, not just a lock.
- **table_feel takes `--max-gc`** (default 4, as check_commander) so its house verdict matches; it passes
  no find-my-combos list, since the fast two-card combos already come through the official reasons.
- **Tests split**: salt summary and share live in `tests/test_table_feel.py` (not `test_scores.py`, which
  the slice-04/05 agent owns); pitch tests in `test_brackets.py`.
