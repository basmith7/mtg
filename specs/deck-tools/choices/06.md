# Slice 06 choices

- Consistency target 89 + MV % (Karsten's per-MV rule), not a flat 90%: lets the computed table be checked
  against his published one; flat 90% would differ by design.
- Land count for the table fixed at 41 (Karsten's), not the deck's own: keeps one citable table; the deck's
  rocks are its "partial lands".
- No mulligan in the hypergeometric: stdlib closed form; Karsten's free-mulligan numbers are listed for MV 1-3.
- Mana check picks the card wanting the most sources (via `sources_needed`), not the most pips: the
  table varies strongly with MV, so 1-pip one-drops can be the binding card.
- Keep rule: 2-5 lands and either 3+ lands or a 2-drop the hand's lands can pay for; no free mulligan
  (not a recorded house rule).
- Land-ramp fetches a land making any colour already on the battlefield (a basic of choice); fetch lands
  with no `produced_mana` count as colourless in the sim.
- Recast is attempted from the turn after the first cast, before ramp; ramp still uses leftover mana.
- Goldfish runs 12 turns so recast-by-T(MV+3) exists for MV 9 commanders.
- Golden test values replaced (model change), pinned on a new two-colour fixture library.
