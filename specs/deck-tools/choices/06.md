# Slice 06 choices

- Mana check picks the card wanting the most sources (via `sources_needed`), not the most pips: the
  table varies strongly with MV, so 1-pip one-drops can be the binding card.
- Keep rule: 2-5 lands and either 3+ lands or a 2-drop the hand's lands can pay for; first mulligan free,
  mulligans capped at four (hand of four after the free one).
- Land-ramp fetches a land making any colour already on the battlefield (a basic of choice).
- Recast is attempted from the turn after the first cast, before ramp; ramp still uses leftover mana.
- Goldfish runs 12 turns so recast-by-T(MV+3) exists for MV 9 commanders.
- Golden test values replaced (model change), pinned on a new two-colour fixture library.
- Source table is Karsten's 2022 lookup (coordinator call: free mulligan applies); unpublished cells take the
  nearest published MV for that pip count rather than a computed value. The hypergeometric was deleted.
- Fetch lands count as a source of every colour they can find (a dual, in the sim); generic basic fetchers
  count for the deck's identity, as Karsten counts them.
