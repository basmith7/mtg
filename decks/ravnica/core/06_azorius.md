The cheaper **Core** build of "Azorius Law & Order": fewer rares, same game plan.

**The Azorius Senate** are Ravnica's lawmakers, who win by controlling the rules of combat. The deck locks down the board and wins in the air.

## How it wins
Detain the opponent's best creatures, counter or bounce their answers, and win with fliers while they can't block or attack.

## Mechanics to know
- **Detain:** until your next turn, the target can't attack, block or activate abilities. [[Azorius Arrester]], [[Lyev Skyknight]] and [[Azorius Justiciar]] each have it.
- **Addendum:** [[Sphinx's Insight]], [[Summary Judgment]] and [[Arrester's Admonition]] get better if cast in your main phase, so decide whether you need instant speed.

## Key cards
- [[Lyev Skyknight]] is a flier that detains any nonland permanent.
- [[Elite Guardmage]] gains 3 life and draws a card.
- [[Sphinx of New Prahv]] is a vigilant flier that is costly to target.
- [[Skymark Roc]] bounces small blockers as it attacks.
- [[Detention Sphere]] and [[Lawmage's Binding]] remove or neutralize threats.
- [[Azorius Charm]] is flexible: draw, lifelink, or tuck an attacker.
- [[No More Lies]] counters a spell and exiles it.

## Tips
- Cast detain creatures on your turn so the opponent can't attack into you.
- Keep up [[No More Lies]] or [[Judge's Familiar]] on the key turns.

## Mana
**Lands:** 9 Plains, 9 Island, 4 Azorius Guildgate (enters tapped) and an Azorius Chancery (enters tapped, returns a land you control to your hand).
