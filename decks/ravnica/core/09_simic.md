The cheaper **Core** build of "Simic Combine Labs": fewer rares, same game plan.

**The Simic Combine** are biologists who splice creatures into impossible hybrids. Everything grows with +1/+1 counters.

## How it wins
Grow a board of creatures that keep getting bigger, then use the counters for card draw, fights, and trampling attackers.

## Mechanics to know
- **Evolve:** whenever a creature with greater power or toughness enters under your control, put a +1/+1 counter on this creature. Play creatures in increasing size.
- **Adapt N:** if this creature has no +1/+1 counters, put N on it. It doesn't work again while counters remain.
- **Proliferate:** add another counter of each kind already on chosen permanents. [[Pollenbright Druid]] and [[Merfolk Skydiver]] can do it.

## Key cards
- [[Experiment One]], [[Cloudfin Raptor]], [[Shambleshark]] and [[Crocanura]] are cheap evolve creatures that grow quickly.
- [[Fathom Mage]] draws a card whenever it gets a counter.
- [[Sharktocrab]] taps down a creature whenever it gets counters.
- [[Zegana, Utopian Speaker]] and [[Trollbred Guardian]] give your countered creatures trample.
- [[Incubation Druid]] makes mana, and a lot of it once it has a counter.
- [[Biogenic Upgrade]] places and doubles counters on up to three creatures.

## Tips
- Sequence creatures smallest to largest to maximize evolve.
- [[Mutant's Prey]] and [[Titanic Brawl]] are your removal and want a creature with counters, so set them up.

## Mana
**Lands:** 9 Forest, 9 Island, 4 Simic Guildgate (enters tapped) and a Simic Growth Chamber (enters tapped, returns a land you control to your hand).
