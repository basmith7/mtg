The cheaper **Core** build of "Rakdos Carnival": fewer rares, same game plan.

**The Cult of Rakdos** is the carnival of chaos, where the show never stops. The deck is fast, aggressive and a little reckless.

## How it wins
Curve out with hasty and aggressive creatures, make the opponent lose life early, then use burn and spectacle spells for cheap to finish.

## Mechanics to know
- **Spectacle:** an alternate cost you can pay if an opponent lost life this turn. Attack or ping first, then cast [[Skewer the Critics]] for {R} or [[Light Up the Stage]] for {R}.
- **Unleash:** [[Rakdos Cackler]] can enter with a +1/+1 counter, but then it can't block.

## Key cards
- [[Spike Jester]] is a 3/1 haste for two ({B}{R}), the best opener.
- [[Judith, the Scourge Diva]] gives your team +1/+0 and pings when your creatures die.
- [[Rakdos Firewheeler]] hits the opponent for 2 and a creature for 2.
- [[Rix Maadi Reveler]] refills your hand when cast with spectacle.
- [[Hackrobat]] and [[Spikewheel Acrobat]] are cheap with spectacle.
- [[Lava Coil]], [[Bedevil]] and [[Shock]] clear blockers.
- [[Carnival // Carnage]] pings early or empties the opponent's hand late.

## Tips
- Play creatures first, attack, then spend burn after damage so spectacle is on.
- [[Footlight Fiend]] is a fine chump or trade that still deals 1 damage.

## Mana
**Lands:** 9 Swamp, 9 Mountain, 4 Rakdos Guildgate (enters tapped) and a Rakdos Carnarium (enters tapped, returns a land you control to your hand).
