The cheaper **Core** build of "Gruul Rubblebelt Riot": fewer rares, same game plan.

**The Gruul Clans** are Ravnica's rampaging outcasts who smash everything in their path. The deck is fast, big and mean.

## How it wins
Land efficient creatures on turns one through three, push through damage with tricks, and use fight spells to clear the way.

## Mechanics to know
- **Riot:** this creature enters with your choice of a +1/+1 counter or haste. Take haste to attack now, or the counter for a lasting body.
- **Bloodrush:** discard this card from your hand to pump an attacking creature. [[Ghor-Clan Rampager]] and [[Zhur-Taa Swine]] double as tricks.

## Key cards
- [[Burning-Tree Emissary]] pays for itself and often a second creature.
- [[Gruul Spellbreaker]] has trample and hexproof for you and itself on your turn.
- [[Clamor Shaman]] stops a blocker every attack.
- [[Ravager Wurm]] fights a creature on arrival.
- [[Rubblebelt Rioters]] hits hard with haste.
- [[Domri's Ambush]], [[Savage Smash]] and [[Pit Fight]] turn your creatures into removal.
- [[Collision // Colossus]] kills a flier or gives +4/+2 and trample.

## Tips
- Lead with cheap riot creatures and decide haste or counter based on the opponent's board.
- Hold [[Ghor-Clan Rampager]] for an unblocked attacker or an opposing blocker.

## Mana
**Lands:** 9 Mountain, 9 Forest, 4 Gruul Guildgate (enters tapped) and a Gruul Turf (enters tapped, returns a land you control to your hand).
