The cheaper **Core** build of "Golgari Swarm": fewer rares, same game plan.

**The Golgari Swarm** are the undercity's scavengers, who grow stronger from whatever dies. The deck turns its own graveyard into an army.

## How it wins
Fill the graveyard, then cast creatures that scale off it, and trade removal for the opponent's best threats. Recursion keeps the value coming.

## Mechanics to know
- **Undergrowth:** counts the creature cards in your graveyard. [[Rhizome Lurcher]] and [[Golgari Raiders]] enter with that many counters, and [[Izoni, Thousand-Eyed]] makes that many Insects.
- **Mill:** [[Glowspore Shaman]], [[Grisly Salvage]] and [[Sludge Titan]] fill the graveyard and dig for creatures.

## Key cards
- [[Glowspore Shaman]] mills three and can put a land back on top.
- [[Kraul Harpooner]] has reach and can fight a flyer on arrival.
- [[Sludge Titan]] is a trampling finisher that digs every time it attacks.
- [[Izoni, Thousand-Eyed]] turns your graveyard into bodies and sacrifices them for cards.
- [[Golgari Findbroker]] returns a permanent from the graveyard to your hand.
- [[Putrefy]], [[Murder]] and [[Deathsprout]] handle creatures. [[Status // Statue]] is flexible removal or a combat trick.
- [[Find // Finality]] gets back two creatures, or sweeps the board.

## Tips
- Wait a turn or two before casting undergrowth creatures so they come in bigger.
- [[Pitiless Gorgon]] holds the ground with deathtouch while you set up.

## Mana
**Lands:** 9 Swamp, 9 Forest, 4 Golgari Guildgate (enters tapped) and a Golgari Rot Farm (enters tapped, returns a land you control to your hand).
