The cheaper **Core** build of "Selesnya Conclave": fewer rares, same game plan.

**The Selesnya Conclave** is a nature cult where everyone joins the collective. The deck goes wide with tokens and makes the whole army bigger.

## How it wins
Make Centaurs and Soldiers, pump them all at once, and swarm. It's resilient against removal because nearly every card is a body.

## Mechanics to know
- **Convoke:** tap your creatures to help pay a spell's cost. Each creature pays {1} or one mana of its color. Cast big spells early by tapping tokens.
- **Populate:** create a copy of a creature token you already control. [[Coursers' Accord]] makes a Centaur and then copies your best token.

## Key cards
- [[Trostani Discordant]] pumps the team and makes two lifelink Soldiers.
- [[Call of the Conclave]] is a 3/3 for three, the backbone of the token plan.
- [[March of the Multitudes]] is an instant convoke army, at the end of the opponent's turn or as a surprise block.
- [[Conclave Tribunal]] is convoke removal that often costs almost nothing.
- [[Worldsoul Colossus]] and [[Venerated Loxodon]] are convoke finishers that grow the team.
- [[Selesnya Charm]] pumps, exiles a big creature, or makes a Knight.
- [[Flower // Flourish]] fetches a land early, or gives the team +2/+2 for the kill.

## Tips
- Don't overextend into a likely board wipe if you can hold [[Selesnya Charm]] back.
- [[Pledge of Unity]] is best after you've built a wide board.

## Mana
**Lands:** 9 Forest, 9 Plains, 4 Selesnya Guildgate (enters tapped) and a Selesnya Sanctuary (enters tapped, returns a land you control to your hand).
