The cheaper **Core** build of "Orzhov Syndicate": fewer rares, same game plan.

**The Orzhov Syndicate** is the church and the bank, where every debt gets collected. The deck drains the opponent a little at a time.

## How it wins
Play fliers and sticky creatures, pay extort on every spell, and gain life while the opponent's total ticks down.

## Mechanics to know
- **Extort:** whenever you cast a spell, you may pay {W/B} to drain each opponent for 1. [[Tithe Drinker]], [[Kingpin's Pet]] and [[Basilica Screecher]] have it, and they stack.
- **Afterlife:** when this creature dies, make 1/1 flying Spirits. [[Ministrant of Obligation]], [[Imperious Oligarch]] and [[Seraph of the Scales]] leave bodies behind.

## Key cards
- [[Tithe Drinker]] is a lifelinking extort creature.
- [[Teysa Karlov]] doubles death triggers like afterlife and gives tokens lifelink.
- [[Seraph of the Scales]] is a flier that picks up deathtouch or vigilance.
- [[Sorin, Vengeful Bloodlord]] gives lifelink on your turn and returns creatures.
- [[Mortify]], [[Murder]] and [[Despark]] are your removal.
- [[Debt to the Deathless]] is a late-game drain for the finish.
- [[Gift of Orzhova]] turns any creature into a flying lifelinker.

## Tips
- Play your extort creatures early and pay extort whenever you have spare mana.
- Trade freely: afterlife gives you something back when creatures die.

## Mana
**Lands:** 9 Plains, 9 Swamp, 4 Orzhov Guildgate (enters tapped) and an Orzhov Basilica (enters tapped, returns a land you control to your hand).
