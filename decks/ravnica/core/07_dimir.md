The cheaper **Core** build of "Dimir Spies & Secrets": fewer rares, same game plan.

**The House Dimir** are Ravnica's spies, who win through information and quiet, evasive threats.

## How it wins
Surveil to smooth your draws, grow small evasive creatures, and back them with cheap removal and counterspells.

## Mechanics to know
- **Surveil N:** look at the top N cards of your library and put any into the graveyard. [[Dimir Informant]], [[Watcher in the Mist]], [[Notion Rain]] and others all surveil.
- **Surveil payoffs:** [[Dimir Spybug]] and [[Thoughtbound Phantasm]] grow, [[Whispering Snitch]] drains, and [[Darkblade Agent]] gets deathtouch.

## Key cards
- [[Thief of Sanity]] steals the best card from the opponent every hit.
- [[Dimir Spybug]] and [[Nightveil Sprite]] are evasive threats that surveil.
- [[Watcher in the Mist]] is a flier that surveils 2.
- [[Tyrant's Scorn]], [[Murder]] and [[Deadly Visit]] deal with creatures.
- [[Sinister Sabotage]] counters anything. [[Dimir Charm]] counters, kills or digs.
- [[Thought Erasure]] strips the best card from a hand.
- [[Forbidden Alchemy]] digs four deep.

## Tips
- Curve small fliers early, then hold up a counterspell.
- Use surveil to find lands early and to bin spare ones later.

## Mana
**Lands:** 9 Island, 9 Swamp, 4 Dimir Guildgate (enters tapped) and a Dimir Aqueduct (enters tapped, returns a land you control to your hand).
