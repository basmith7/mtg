The cheaper **Core** build of "Boros Legion": fewer rares, same game plan.

**The Boros Legion** is Ravnica's army and police force: disciplined soldiers who win by attacking together, every single turn.

## How it wins
Flood the board with cheap creatures, grow them with mentor, and finish with burn. Most games end by turn 6 or 7.

## Mechanics to know
- **Mentor:** when this creature attacks, put a +1/+1 counter on target attacking creature with lesser power. Attack with mentors alongside small creatures.

## Key cards
- [[Legion Warboss]] makes a hasty Goblin every combat that must attack, and mentors it.
- [[Aurelia, Exemplar of Justice]] gives a creature +2/+0 each combat, plus trample or vigilance depending on its color.
- [[Truefire Captain]] deals damage to a player whenever it's dealt damage, so blocking it hurts.
- [[Lightning Helix]] is 3 damage and 3 life, for a blocker or for the face.
- [[Integrity // Intervention]] and [[Response // Resurgence]] are flexible tricks: pump, burn, kill an attacker, or take an extra combat.
- [[Sunhome Guildmage]] pumps the team or makes hasty Soldiers when you have spare mana late.

## Tips
- Attack every turn. Your creatures are worse on defense than theirs.
- Save [[Lightning Helix]] for blockers early, and for the opponent's face late.
- [[Luminous Bonds]] neutralizes the one blocker that stops your attack.

## Mana
**Lands:** 9 Mountain, 9 Plains, 4 Boros Guildgate (enters tapped) and a Boros Garrison (enters tapped, returns a land you control to your hand). Play the tapped lands on turns where you have nothing else to cast.
