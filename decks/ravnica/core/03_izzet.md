The cheaper **Core** build of "Izzet Spellstorm": fewer rares, same game plan.

**The Izzet League** are mad scientists who turn every spell into a spectacle. The deck casts a pile of cheap instants and sorceries and lets its creatures feed on them.

## How it wins
Chain cheap spells to pump small creatures, burn the opponent's board, and fly over for the win.

## Mechanics to know
- **Jump-start:** cast this card from your graveyard by discarding a card, then exile it. [[Radical Idea]], [[Direct Current]] and [[Chemister's Insight]] give you two uses each.
- **Spell payoffs:** many creatures trigger on instants and sorceries, so sequence cheap spells after the creature is on the table.

## Key cards
- [[Goblin Electromancer]] makes your spells cheaper so you can cast several per turn.
- [[Wee Dragonauts]] and [[Blistercoil Weird]] get bigger every time you cast an instant or sorcery.
- [[Murmuring Mystic]] makes a flying Bird for every instant or sorcery.
- [[Crackling Drake]] draws a card and grows with your graveyard.
- [[Arclight Phoenix]] returns from the graveyard if you cast three spells in a turn.
- [[Ral, Izzet Viceroy]] digs for spells and kills creatures using your graveyard.
- [[Electrolyze]] kills small creatures and draws a card.

## Tips
- Hold [[Izzet Charm]] for a key noncreature spell, or use it to loot early.
- Keep burn for blockers and creatures, not just the face, until you're ready to race.

## Mana
**Lands:** 9 Island, 9 Mountain, 4 Izzet Guildgate (enters tapped) and an Izzet Boilerworks (enters tapped, returns a land you control to your hand).
