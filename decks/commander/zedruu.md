Zedruu gives your stuff to the table and pays you for it: every upkeep you gain life and draw a card for each permanent you own that an opponent controls. This deck fills the table with gifts, some generous and some cursed, and then cashes in.

## How it plays
- **Early:** rocks ([[Sol Ring]], three Signets, two Talismans, [[Thought Vessel]]) and cheap gifts. [[Humble Defector]] draws two cards and hands itself to an opponent, which counts for Zedruu straight away. [[Goblin Cadets]] gives itself away whenever it blocks.
- **Mid:** cast Zedruu on turn 4. Pay {U}{R}{W} to hand a permanent to whoever needs a friend. Peace offerings ([[Propaganda]], [[Windborn Muse]], [[Pramikon, Sky Rampart]]) keep you alive while the gifts pile up.
- **Late:** the table owns half your board, your hand is full and your life total is huge. Time to spend it.

## How it wins
- **Life as a weapon:** [[Aetherflux Reservoir]] turns the life Zedruu gains into 50 damage. [[Alhammarret's Archive]] doubles both the life and the cards.
- **Cursed parting gifts:** give an opponent [[Illusions of Grandeur]] (they lose 20 when it leaves) or [[Delusions of Mediocrity]] (10). They pay upkeep or take it.
- **Draw-based:** [[Jace, Wielder of Mysteries]] wins when you draw from an empty library; [[Approach of the Second Sun]] wins on its second cast.
- None of these is an instant win: Aetherflux and Approach need a lot of life or mana.

## Key cards
- [[Zedruu the Greathearted]]: {U}{R}{W}: target opponent gains control of target permanent you control. At your upkeep, gain X life and draw X, where X is the permanents you own that opponents control.
- [[Akroan Horse]]: an opponent gains control as it enters; at your upkeep each opponent makes a 1/1 Soldier. Gift that keeps giving.
- [[Steel Golem]]: you can't cast creature spells. Cast it, give it away, now it's their problem.
- [[Jinxed Choker]]: at your end step an opponent takes it and adds a charge counter. It hurts whoever has it at upkeep.
- [[Crown of Doom]]: attackers get +2/+0 against you; {2}: give it to another player on your turn.
- [[Harmless Offering]] and [[Donate]]: the cheap, reliable ways to hand over anything.
- [[Role Reversal]]: exchange two permanents of the same type, a gift and a theft in one card.
- [[Cyclonic Rift]]: overloaded ({6}{U}), it returns every nonland permanent you don't control to hand, including your gifts.

## Tips
- Gift what the table needs, not just what hurts. Keep one friend at the table fed and they'll stop attacking you.
- Cards you own that opponents control also keep counting: give away [[Rust Elemental]] and [[Cinder Giant]] early.
- Watch the cursed ones that hurt you: [[Goldnight Castigator]] doubles damage dealt to you, so use it as a gift.
- Four Game Changers: [[Rhystic Study]], [[Smothering Tithe]], [[Cyclonic Rift]], [[Teferi's Protection]].
- Mulligan for 2+ lands and a rock. Zedruu wants mana, not a perfect hand.
