Jon Irenicus hands out "gifts": demons that eat their owner's life, djinn that burn whoever holds them, and a creature that makes its controller lose the game. Every gift is goaded, so it attacks every turn, and every one of those attacks draws you a card.

## How it plays
- **Early:** Ramp with rocks, set up draw ([[Rhystic Study]], [[Mystic Remora]]), and drop cheap drawbacks like [[Sleeper Agent]], [[Serendib Efreet]] and [[Juzám Djinn]]. Their downside doesn't matter, because you won't be holding them for long.
- **Mid:** Cast Jon and give away one creature each end step. It gets two +1/+1 counters, can't be sacrificed, and has to attack someone other than you. Each attack draws you a card. Use the exchange cards ([[Gilded Drake]], [[Switcheroo]], [[Shifting Grift]], [[Puca's Mischief]]) to trade a cursed demon for their best creature.
- **Late:** Your hand is full and the table is beating itself up with your creatures. Steal the best threats with [[Agent of Treachery]] and [[Treachery]], then finish with an X drain.

## How it wins
- **Gift damage:** [[Lord of the Pit]] and [[Liege of the Pit]] make their controller sacrifice another creature each upkeep or take 7. [[Ebonblade Reaper]] halves its controller's life every time it attacks, and Jon makes sure it attacks.
- **[[Archfiend of the Dross]]:** give it away and its controller loses the game when the last oil counter comes off.
- **Drain:** [[Exsanguinate]] and [[Torment of Hailfire]] turn all the mana and cards you've piled up into a finish. [[Blood Artist]] drains whenever someone's creatures trade with your gifts.
- **Combat:** stolen fatties plus your own [[Coveted Falcon]] and [[Priest of the Blood Rite]] Demon token.
- No combos. It's a value engine that closes with damage and drain.

## Key cards
- [[Jon Irenicus, Shattered One]]: gives away one creature at your end step, and draws you a card whenever a creature you own but don't control attacks.
- [[Coveted Falcon]]: whenever it attacks, gain control of target permanent you own but don't control. Take back a gift that has grown, or one that has stopped hurting.
- [[Fateful Handoff]]: draw cards equal to a creature's mana value, then an opponent gains control of it. A 7-drop Lord of the Pit is a big draw.
- [[Priest of the Blood Rite]]: you keep the 5/5 flying Demon and give away the Priest, along with its 2 life loss each upkeep.
- [[Witch Engine]]: tap it for {B}{B}{B}{B}, then target opponent gains control of it. Free ramp before you give it away.
- [[Cosmic Horror]]: its controller pays {3}{B}{B}{B} each upkeep, or it's destroyed and deals 7 damage to them. Destroy still works on a creature that can't be sacrificed.
- [[Agent of Treachery]]: steals any permanent and draws three at your end step if you control three or more permanents you don't own.
- [[Bident of Thassa]]: {1}{U}, tap: all your opponents' creatures attack this turn, including your gifts, which draws you cards from Jon.

## Tips
- Give gifts to the player who can least afford them, and say so out loud. "The demon goes to whoever attacks me" is a strong deal.
- Goad makes gifts attack your opponents, not you, so a gift is never a threat to you unless you take it back.
- [[Cyclonic Rift]] overloaded returns your gifts to **your** hand, so you can give them away again.
- Gifts that die go to your graveyard. [[Animate Dead]] and [[Reanimate]] bring them back so you can give them away again.
- Mulligan for 2+ lands, a rock, and one cheap drawback creature or a draw engine.
