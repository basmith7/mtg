Tivit runs a crooked election every time it enters or connects. Every vote pays you either Clues or Treasures. The deck stacks extra votes, plays a dozen other council cards, and flickers Tivit to hold the election again. The table is always voting on something, and you usually decide how it turns out.

## How it plays
- **Early:** Make land drops and cast rocks ([[Sol Ring]], Signets, Talismans). Set up draw engines ([[Rhystic Study]], [[Esper Sentinel]]) and play cheap voters like [[Grudge Keeper]] and [[Tyrant's Choice]].
- **Mid:** Cast Tivit on turn 4 or 5. Ward {3} makes it expensive to remove. You vote twice, so each election gives you at least two tokens, and each opponent adds one more. [[Ballot Broker]] and [[Brago's Representative]] each give you another vote. That lets you outvote the table on will-of-the-council cards like [[Coercive Portal]], [[Magister of Worth]] and [[Council's Judgment]].
- **Late:** Flicker Tivit with [[Ephemerate]], [[Thassa, Deep-Dwelling]], [[Conjurer's Closet]], [[Brago, King Eternal]] or [[Abuelo, Ancestral Echo]]. Turn the pile of tokens into cards, a big [[Expropriate]], or one of the wins below.

## How it wins
- **Treasure pile:** [[Revel in Riches]] wins at your upkeep if you control ten or more Treasures. Two Tivit triggers usually get you there. [[Mechanized Production]] on a Treasure or Clue wins once you control eight artifacts with the same name.
- **Sacrifice drain:** [[Disciple of the Vault]] and [[Marionette Apprentice]] make opponents lose life whenever you crack a Treasure or Clue. A board full of tokens drains everyone out quickly.
- **Air force:** Tivit, [[Magister of Worth]], [[Restoration Angel]], [[Custodi Squire]] and [[Teysa, Opulent Oligarch]]'s Spirits beat down in the air. Meanwhile [[Grudge Keeper]] and [[Tyrant's Choice]] chip in damage.
- **Late-game combo (be upfront about it):** With [[Deadeye Navigator]] paired with Tivit, each flicker costs {1}{U}. Your two bribery votes pay that back in Treasures, and each opponent's vote is extra profit. Add [[Ballot Broker]] or [[Brago's Representative]] and the loop makes infinite mana and tokens. That wins with Revel in Riches, Disciple or Marionette Apprentice. Tivit costs 6 and Navigator costs 5, so this is a late-game finisher, not an early one.

## Key cards
- [[Ballot Broker]] / [[Brago's Representative]]: you get an extra vote while voting.
- [[Illusion of Choice]]: {U}, you choose how each player votes this turn, then draw a card. Cast it before Tivit or [[Expropriate]].
- [[Expropriate]]: each time vote is an extra turn, and each money vote steals a permanent from that voter. It's the only extra-turn card in the deck.
- [[Council's Judgment]]: each player votes for a nonland permanent you don't control, and the ones with the most votes are exiled. It gets around hexproof.
- [[Coercive Portal]]: draws a card each upkeep. If carnage gets more votes, sacrifice the Portal and destroy all nonland permanents. Your extra votes let you choose which.
- [[Magister of Worth]]: grace returns every creature card from every graveyard to the battlefield. Condemnation, or a tie, destroys all other creatures.
- [[Grudge Keeper]]: after each vote, every opponent who voted differently from you loses 2 life.
- [[Deadeye Navigator]]: soulbond. Paired creatures get "{1}{U}: exile this creature, then return it."
- [[Erdwal Illuminator]]: the first time you investigate each turn, you investigate an extra time.

## Tips
- Use the votes for politics. Bribery Treasures help you, but evidence Clues are slower. Offer to go easy on a player who votes your way.
- Hold flicker spells as instant-speed answers to removal. [[Ephemerate]] saves Tivit and makes it vote again.
- [[Coercive Portal]] and [[Magister of Worth]] are wipes you control. Choose carnage or condemnation when you're behind on board.
- Mulligan for two or more lands, a mana rock and one draw engine. Tivit is easy to cast on curve, so you don't need to keep a hand built around it.
