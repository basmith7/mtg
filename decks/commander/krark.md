A mono-red-leaning Izzet slot machine. Every instant or sorcery you cast is a coin flip: tails and it goes back to your hand, heads and it is copied. The rest of the deck hands the table coin flips and random free spells too, so no two games play alike.

## How it plays
- **Early:** cheap rocks, [[Sol Ring]] and cantrips like [[Ponder]] and [[Preordain]], then [[Krark, the Thumbless]] on turn 2. Bolt a creature and see what the coin says.
- **Mid:** [[Sakashima of a Thousand Faces]] copies Krark, which means two flips per spell and a good chance that a spell either comes back or doubles. Engines like [[Storm-Kiln Artist]], [[Young Pyromancer]] and [[Guttersnipe]] turn every flip into value.
- **Late:** big [[Fireball]] or [[Comet Storm]] turns, fed by rituals ([[Pyretic Ritual]], [[Seething Song]], [[Jeska's Will]]).

## How it wins
- **Burn out:** Fireball and Comet Storm, copied by winning flips, plus Guttersnipe and [[Thermo-Alchemist]] pings.
- **Luck:** [[Chance Encounter]] wins at ten luck counters, one per coin flip won, and with Krark on board that arrives faster than it sounds.
- **Reservoir:** [[Aetherflux Reservoir]] turns a long chain of spells into 50 life and 50 damage.
- **Combos:** the checker lists exactly one included combo, [[Brash Taunter]] + [[Aetherflux Reservoir]] (infinite damage), and it needs lifelink on Taunter and 51 life. The deck has no way to give lifelink, so treat it as a curiosity, not a plan. It is not FAST.

## Key cards
- [[Krark's Thumb]]: you flip two coins and ignore one, so Krark's flips and every other flip in the deck get better.
- [[Possibility Storm]]: when anyone casts a spell from hand, they exile it and cast a random same-type card from their library for free. Everyone plays it.
- [[Fickle Efreet]]: if you lose the end-of-combat flip, an opponent gains control of it. Free gifts for the table.
- [[Puppet's Verdict]]: heads kills power 2 or less, tails kills power 3 or more. A one-sided or all-sided sweeper depending on the board.
- [[Wheel of Fortune]]: each player discards their hand, then draws seven.
- [[Mana Clash]]: you and an opponent flip until both hit heads together, with 1 damage to each tails. Silly, and with Thumb, very quick.
- [[Okaun, Eye of Chaos]]: flips at combat until it loses and doubles whenever any player wins a flip.
- [[Fork]]: copies an instant or sorcery. Good insurance after a lost flip.

## Tips
- **Sakashima:** the legend rule doesn't apply to your permanents, so a second Krark is fine and a copy of an opponent's creature is fun. Hold it until Krark is out or you can copy something better.
- Cast the cheap spell first. A tails flip costs you only mana, so use cantrips and rituals to test the coin before the big spell.
- A tails flip returns the spell but not the mana. Count what you have left before you commit a big X spell.
- Mulligan for 2-4 lands, one engine or cantrip, and Krark or a rock.
- Save [[Counterspell]] and [[Cyclonic Rift]] for the threat that actually ends the game; the burn will keep the table honest.
