A heist deck. Small, sneaky creatures connect, and every hit lets you play a card off the top of that player's library. You win with everyone else's cards, and you pay less for them.

## How it plays
- **Early:** ramp, then cheap evasive bodies: [[Slither Blade]], [[Triton Shorestalker]], [[Invisible Stalker]], [[Dauthi Voidwalker]], [[Changeling Outcast]]. Don't wait for the commander to start stealing.
- **Mid:** [[Gonti, Canny Acquisitor]] makes every creature you control a thief, and your stolen spells cost {1} less. Add the other thieves ([[Thief of Sanity]], [[Hostage Taker]], [[Gonti, Lord of Luxury]]) and keep hitting.
- **Late:** you cast the table's best cards with any mana. Use [[Villainous Wealth]] and [[Kotis, the Fangkeeper]] to cast a pile of them free, or take their best creature with [[Bribery]] or [[Mind Flayer]].

## How it wins
- **Evasive beats:** 12 creatures have flying, fear, shadow, menace, islandwalk or can't be blocked. [[Rogue's Passage]], [[Access Tunnel]] and [[Predators' Hour]] help the rest connect.
- **Pump the team:** [[Overwhelming Stampede]] adds the greatest power among your creatures to each of your creatures and gives them trample.
- **Poison:** [[Triumph of the Hordes]] gives your creatures +1/+1, trample and infect until end of turn. A wide board of thieves can reach 10 poison in one swing.
- **Their own cards:** stolen threats and free spells from [[Villainous Wealth]] often end the game.
- There are no infinite combos, so the game ends through combat.

## Key cards
- [[Gonti, Canny Acquisitor]]: when your creatures deal combat damage to a player, exile that player's top card face down. You may play it and spend mana of any type. Spells you cast but don't own cost {1} less.
- [[Gonti, Night Minister]]: whenever any player casts a spell they don't own, they make a Treasure. Whenever any creature deals combat damage to one of your opponents, its controller exiles that player's top card and may play it. It works for the whole table, so it can help the opponents too.
- [[Thief of Sanity]]: flying. On combat damage, look at their top three cards, exile one face down and put the others into their graveyard. You may cast it.
- [[Hostage Taker]]: exiles another creature or artifact until it leaves, and you may cast it.
- [[Dauthi Voidwalker]]: shadow. Opponents' cards that would hit the graveyard are exiled with void counters. Sacrifice it to play one free.
- [[Opposition Agent]]: flash. You control opponents while they search, and their found cards are exiled for you to play. It beats tutors and fetch lands.
- [[Kotis, the Fangkeeper]]: indestructible. On combat damage, exile that many cards from the top of their library and cast any with mana value X or less free.
- [[Tergrid, God of Fright // Tergrid's Lantern]]: menace. When opponents sacrifice or discard, you take the card. Its Lantern side makes them choose between losing life, sacrificing and discarding.
- [[Edric, Spymaster of Trest]]: any creature that damages an opponent draws its controller a card. It helps everyone, so it is a politics card.

## Tips
- Stealing is the plan, so give people a reason to let you: [[Gonti, Night Minister]] and [[Edric, Spymaster of Trest]] feed the table.
- Take the best card, not the first. [[Hostage Taker]] and [[Control Magic]] are best on the table's biggest creature.
- Keep [[Cyclonic Rift]], [[Counterspell]] and [[Heroic Intervention]] up for a sweeper aimed at your small board.
- Mulligan for 3+ lands and an early evasive creature or ramp piece.
- Track exiled cards on a notepad or in a window. You can play them for as long as they stay exiled.
