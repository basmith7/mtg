Anzrag gets an extra combat every time it becomes blocked, and this deck makes sure it always is. [[Invasion Plans]] forces every creature to block and lets you assign the blocks. Red's blocker-punishing enchantments turn each forced block into a board wipe or burn.

## How it plays
- **Early:** Ramp with elves, Signets and land searches. [[Neyith of the Dire Hunt]] draws off forced blocks.
- **Mid:** Cast Anzrag on turn 3 (about 58% of games) or turn 4 (about 93%). Before you attack with it, set up a forced block: [[Invasion Plans]], [[Lure]], [[Nemesis Mask]] or [[Irresistible Prey]]. Protect it with [[Dolmen Gate]], [[Darksteel Plate]], [[Tamiyo's Safekeeping]] or Boots. As an 8/4, it dies to a big blocker if nothing protects it.
- **Late:** Chain combats. Each combat untaps your team, so [[Moraug, Fury of Akoum]], [[Hellrider]], [[Savage Ventmaw]] and [[Old Gnawbone]] pay out again and again.

## How it wins
- **Chained combats:** Anzrag is blocked, your creatures untap, and you attack again. [[Craterhoof Behemoth]] or [[Embercleave]] ends it quickly.
- **Blocker wipes:** With Invasion Plans out, attack with a cheap creature or [[Stuffy Doll]] and pile every blocker onto it ([[Stuffy Doll]] is indestructible). [[Heat Stroke]] destroys them all at end of combat, and [[Battle Strain]] deals 1 damage to their controller for each one.
- **Repeating combat (not a listed combo):** Anzrag plus a forced-block effect ([[Invasion Plans]], [[Lure]], [[Nemesis Mask]], [[Roar of Challenge]]) repeats combat until the table runs out of blockers.
- **Late infinite combos:** all need an attacker that can't be blocked or killed, and all cost 9 or more mana in total, so they are late-game.
  - [[Aggravated Assault]] + [[Old Gnawbone]]: needs creatures that can deal at least six unblockable combat damage.
  - [[Old Gnawbone]] + [[Hellkite Charger]]: needs both dragons unblockable, and Gnawbone without summoning sickness.
  - [[Savage Ventmaw]] + [[Aggravated Assault]]: needs Ventmaw without summoning sickness, and no opponent able to block and kill it.

## Key cards
- [[Invasion Plans]]: all creatures block each combat if able, and the attacking player chooses how each one blocks.
- [[Anzrag, the Quake-Mole]]: whenever it becomes blocked, untap each creature you control, and there is an additional combat phase after this one.
- [[Heat Stroke]]: at end of combat, destroy each creature that blocked or was blocked this turn. **That includes Anzrag**, so give it indestructible first.
- [[No Quarter]]: destroys blockers with less power than the attacker, and the attacker if the blocker has more.
- [[Battle Strain]]: whenever a creature blocks, deals 1 damage to its controller.
- [[Dolmen Gate]]: prevent all combat damage that would be dealt to your attacking creatures.
- [[Lure]] / [[Nemesis Mask]] / [[Taunting Elf]] / [[Elvish Bard]] / [[Roar of Challenge]]: all creatures able to block that creature do so. Roar is a sorcery and adds indestructible if you control power 4 or more.
- [[Aggravated Assault]]: {3}{R}{R}: untap all your creatures, then get an additional combat phase (sorcery speed).
- [[Tamiyo's Safekeeping]]: {G} instant, target permanent you control gains hexproof and indestructible until end of turn, and you gain 2 life.
- [[Neyith of the Dire Hunt]]: draw a card whenever one or more of your creatures fight or become blocked.

## Tips
- **Invasion Plans works for opponents too.** On their turn, they choose how *your* creatures block. Keep [[Heroic Intervention]] up, or leave Plans in hand until you can protect your board.
- Heat Stroke and No Quarter hit your creatures as well. Use Dolmen Gate, [[Darksteel Plate]] or Heroic Intervention before you go wide.
- Attack the player with the most creatures. More blockers means more Battle Strain damage and more chained combats.
- Mulligan for three lands, a ramp piece, and either Invasion Plans or a Lure effect. Anzrag on its own is only an 8/4.
