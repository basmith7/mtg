Anzrag gets an extra combat every time it becomes blocked, and this deck makes sure it always is. [[Invasion Plans]] forces every creature to block and lets you assign the blocks. Then red's old blocker-punishing enchantments turn each forced block into a board wipe or a burn spell. Your opponents' creatures become a resource you spend.

## How it plays
- **Early:** Ramp with elves, Signets and land searches. Play [[Taunting Elf]] or hold [[Irresistible Prey]] to start drawing cards off forced blocks with [[Neyith of the Dire Hunt]].
- **Mid:** Cast Anzrag on turn 3 or 4. Before you attack with it, set up a forced block: [[Invasion Plans]], [[Lure]], [[Nemesis Mask]] or [[Irresistible Prey]]. Late in the game, its own seven-mana ability does the same. Protect it with [[Dolmen Gate]], [[Darksteel Plate]] or Boots. As an 8/4, it dies to a big blocker if nothing protects it.
- **Late:** Chain combats. Each combat untaps your team, so [[Moraug, Fury of Akoum]], [[Hellrider]], [[Savage Ventmaw]] and [[Old Gnawbone]] pay out again and again.

## How it wins
- **Chained combats:** Anzrag is blocked, your creatures untap, and you attack again. Everything that isn't blocking Anzrag connects every combat. [[Craterhoof Behemoth]] or [[Embercleave]] ends it quickly.
- **Blocker wipes:** With Invasion Plans out, attack with a token or [[Stuffy Doll]] and pile every blocker onto it. [[Heat Stroke]] destroys them all at end of combat, and [[Battle Strain]] deals 1 damage to their controller for each one.
- **Stuffy Doll:** It's indestructible and sends any damage it takes to the player you chose. Make a whole board block it.
- **Combo (be upfront about it):** Anzrag plus [[Invasion Plans]], [[Lure]] or [[Nemesis Mask]] repeats combat for as long as Anzrag survives ([[Dolmen Gate]]) and an opponent still has a creature that can block. If one of their blockers survives Anzrag's 8 damage (indestructible, or 9+ toughness), the loop never ends. Usually it just runs until the table has no creatures left. It can come together on turn 5, so tell the table at Rule 0.

## Key cards
- [[Invasion Plans]]: all creatures block each combat if able, and the attacking player chooses how each one blocks.
- [[Anzrag, the Quake-Mole]]: whenever it becomes blocked, untap each creature you control, and there is an additional combat phase after this one.
- [[Heat Stroke]]: at end of combat, destroy each creature that blocked or was blocked this turn. **That includes Anzrag**, so give it indestructible first.
- [[No Quarter]]: blockers with less power than the attacker are destroyed. If the blocker has more power than the attacker, the attacker is destroyed instead.
- [[Battle Strain]]: whenever a creature blocks, deals 1 damage to its controller.
- [[Dolmen Gate]]: prevent all combat damage that would be dealt to your attacking creatures.
- [[Lure]] / [[Nemesis Mask]] / [[Taunting Elf]] / [[Elvish Bard]]: all creatures able to block that creature do so.
- [[Neyith of the Dire Hunt]]: draw a card whenever one or more of your creatures become blocked.
- [[Ohran Frostfang]]: your attackers have deathtouch, and you draw whenever one deals combat damage to a player.
- [[Engulfing Slagwurm]]: destroys whatever blocks it, and you gain life equal to that creature's toughness.

## Tips
- **Invasion Plans works for opponents too.** On their turn, they choose how *your* creatures block. Keep [[Heroic Intervention]] up, or leave Plans in hand until you can protect your board.
- Heat Stroke and No Quarter hit your creatures as well. Use Dolmen Gate, [[Darksteel Plate]] or Heroic Intervention before you go wide.
- Attack the player with the most creatures. More blockers means more Battle Strain damage and more chained combats.
- Mulligan for three lands, a ramp piece, and either Invasion Plans or a Lure effect. Anzrag on its own is only an 8/4.
