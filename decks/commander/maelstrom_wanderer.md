Maelstrom Wanderer is an 8-mana slot machine: cast it, flip two free spells, and give everything haste. This deck ramps hard, stacks extra cascade, and fills the library with big hasty threats and removal, so almost every pull does something you'll remember.

## How it plays
- **Early (turns 1–4):** Ramp. Land spells, [[Birds of Paradise]], [[Lotus Cobra]] and [[Sol Ring]] aim to get Wanderer out on turn 5 or 6. [[Bloodbraid Elf]] and [[Shardless Agent]] give you small cascades along the way.
- **Mid (turns 5–7):** Play a cascade engine like [[Imoti, Celebrant of Bounty]] or [[Quandrix, the Proof]], or one big threat, then cast Wanderer. Its two cascades hit cards that cost 7 or less, such as [[Etali, Primal Conqueror // Etali, Primal Sickness]], [[Avenger of Zendikar]], [[Inferno Titan]] and [[Hornet Queen]]. They all attack right away.
- **Late:** Recast Wanderer from the command zone, cast [[Apex Devastator]] for four cascades, and swing with a hasty board. [[Volcanic Torrent]] cleans up blockers on a turn with lots of spells.

The deck has almost no cheap counterspells or situational instants. Free spells from cascade have X = 0, so there are no X spells either. Almost every hit is a creature, a ramp spell, card draw, or removal that has a target.

## How it wins
- **Hasty beats:** Wanderer plus two big creatures, all attacking that turn. Dragons ([[Thundermaw Hellkite]], [[Stormbreath Dragon]], [[Hellkite Tyrant]]) end games quickly.
- **[[Terror of the Peaks]]:** every creature that enters deals damage equal to its power to any target. A Wanderer turn with Terror out often kills a player.
- **Tokens:** [[Avenger of Zendikar]] and [[Hornet Queen]] attack right away thanks to haste.
- **Poison:** [[Etali, Primal Conqueror // Etali, Primal Sickness]] can transform late and give out poison counters.
- No infinite combos.

## Key cards
- **[[Maelstrom Wanderer]]:** creatures you control have haste; cascade, cascade.
- **[[Imoti, Celebrant of Bounty]]:** has cascade, and your spells with mana value 6 or greater have cascade, so Wanderer cascades three times.
- **[[Quandrix, the Proof]]:** flying, trample, cascade. Instants and sorceries you cast from your hand have cascade, so even [[Cultivate]] gives you a free spell.
- **[[Apex Devastator]]:** cascade four times. Its mana value is 10, so it can hit anything in the deck.
- **[[Sunbird's Invocation]]:** whenever you cast a spell from your hand, reveal the top X cards (X = its mana value) and cast one with mana value X or less for free.
- **[[Averna, the Chaos Bloom]]:** as you cascade, you may put a land from among the exiled cards onto the battlefield tapped. Ramp on every flip.
- **[[Wild-Magic Sorcerer]]:** the first spell you cast from exile each turn has cascade. Spells you cascade into count, so it chains.
- **[[Etali, Primal Conqueror // Etali, Primal Sickness]]:** each player exiles cards from the top of their library until they exile a nonland card, and you may cast all of them for free.
- **[[Hullbreaker Horror]]:** flash, can't be countered. Whenever you cast a spell, you may bounce a spell you don't control or a nonland permanent. A Wanderer turn can bounce three things.

## Tips
- **Sequencing:** cast engines (Imoti, Quandrix, Sunbird, Averna, Wild-Magic) before Wanderer. Cast Wanderer before combat so the creatures you flip can attack.
- **Mulligan** hands without ramp. You want 3+ lands and a ramp piece; aim for 6–8 mana by turn 5.
- **Politics:** one cascade turn makes you the threat. Be generous with the first hasty attacks, aim at whoever is ahead, and keep [[Cyclonic Rift]] or [[Koma, Cosmos Serpent]] up to protect yourself afterward.
- [[Throes of Chaos]] has retrace: discard spare lands late to cascade again.
- Removal (Beast Within, Chaos Warp, Pongify, Rapid Hybridization) can hit your own token if nothing else is worth it, since cascade casts are optional.
