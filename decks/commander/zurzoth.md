[[Zurzoth, Chaos Rider]] turns the whole table's card draw into your army, then sends that army to make everyone draw more. Every opponent who draws on your turn hands you a Devil, and every Devil that attacks makes you and its victim draw and discard at random. It is a mono-red deck that plays for and with the table.

## How it plays
**Early:** [[Sol Ring]], [[Mind Stone]] and [[Fire Diamond]] get Zurzoth down on turn 3. Cheap Devils like [[Witty Roastmaster]], [[Tibalt's Rager]] and [[Spiteful Prankster]] fill the gaps.

**Mid:** the table draws on your turn: [[Temple Bell]], [[Mikokoro, Center of the Sea]], [[Geier Reach Sanitarium]] and [[Howling Golem]] each make every opponent draw, and Zurzoth pays you a Devil for the first one. Attack with the Devils and everyone they hit draws and discards at random. The deck has 22 creatures, 11 of them Devils (counting Zurzoth), and 4 more cards that make Devil tokens.

**Late:** wheel the table with [[Wheel of Fortune]], [[Reforge the Soul]] or [[Dragon Mage]], then use the burst of Devils and ETB triggers to finish. [[Maskwood Nexus]] makes every creature you control a Devil, so [[Siege-Gang Commander]]'s Goblins attack as Devils too.

## How it wins
- **Pings:** [[Impact Tremors]], [[Purphoros, God of the Forge]], [[Hellrider]] and [[Witty Roastmaster]] hurt every opponent per creature, and [[Torbran, Thane of Red Fell]] adds 2 to each ping.
- **Death pings:** [[Goblin Bombardment]] sacrifices Devils to deal 1 damage each, and each Devil token also pings on death.
- **Big burn:** [[Fireball]], [[Comet Storm]] and [[Banefire]], fuelled by [[Jeska's Will]].
- **Fliers and Titans:** [[Terror of the Peaks]] and [[Inferno Titan]].
- **Combos:** the checker lists none in this deck.

## Key cards
- [[Temple Bell]]: each player draws a card. Tap it on your turn and every opponent draws off-turn for Zurzoth.
- [[Humble Defector]]: you draw two, then an opponent gains control of it. It does the same for them on their turn, so it travels around the table.
- [[Scrawling Crawler]]: each player draws at your upkeep and each opponent loses 1 life per card they draw.
- [[Razorkin Needlehead]]: deals 1 damage to an opponent whenever they draw.
- [[Burning Inquiry]]: each player draws three, then discards three at random.
- [[Wheel of Misfortune]]: everyone secretly picks a number, so it is a bluffing game that costs the highest bidders life.
- [[Possibility Storm]]: whenever a player casts a spell from hand, they exile it and cast a random card of the same type from their library instead.
- [[Hellrider]]: a Devil that pings for every creature you attack with.

## Tips
- Zurzoth's token trigger needs the opponent's first draw of the turn, and it is not their turn. Draw spells in your own turn's main phase and your Devils will show up.
- Zurzoth's attack trigger hits you too. Make sure your hand is full of cards you can lose.
-
- Keep [[Blasphemous Act]] for when you are behind; with Devils out it also pings.
- Mulligan for a rock, 3 lands and one cheap Devil or draw engine.
- [[Gamble]] and [[Jeska's Will]] are the deck's 2 Game Changers. Gamble finds a payoff and discards at random, so hold a spare card.
