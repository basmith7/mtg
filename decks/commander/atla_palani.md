Atla Palani makes 0/1 Eggs, and every time one dies you reveal cards until you hit a creature and put it onto the battlefield. This deck fills the library with 34 other creatures, most of them enormous, then kills its own Eggs on purpose and sees what hatches.

## How it plays
- **Early:** Ramp with [[Sol Ring]], [[Wall of Roots]], [[Overgrown Battlement]] and [[Axebane Guardian]]. Your Eggs and walls have defender, so the Battlement and Guardian get silly fast.
- **Mid:** Atla comes down and makes two mana worth of Eggs per turn. Add a sacrifice outlet ([[Ashnod's Altar]], [[Phyrexian Altar]], [[Goblin Bombardment]], [[High Market]], [[Skullclamp]]) and every Egg becomes a free reveal. [[Palani's Hatcher]] and [[Nesting Dragon]] make more Eggs on their own.
- **Late:** Hatch Dragonlord Atarka, Worldspine Wurm or Zacama for free. Kill your own board on purpose with [[Blasphemous Act]]: every Egg that dies is a reveal.

## How it wins
- **Big stuff:** The reveals and cheats ([[Elvish Piper]], [[Sneak Attack]], [[Natural Order]]) put fatties out early, and [[Terror of the Peaks]] or [[Warstorm Surge]] turn each arrival into damage.
- **Combat and pings:** [[Craterhoof Behemoth]] and [[Mirror Entity]] make a wide board of Eggs lethal, and [[Goblin Bombardment]] turns each Egg into 1 damage plus a free creature.
- **Combo (late):** Atla + [[Mirror Entity]] + [[Worldspine Wurm]]: Mirror Entity for X=0 kills every creature you control but Atla, and each death triggers Atla. It loops infinitely, but it needs a way to raise only Atla's toughness, which this deck has no dedicated card for. Treat it as a rare accident, not a plan.

## Key cards
- [[Annie Joins Up]]: deals 5 damage to a creature or planeswalker, and triggered abilities of your legendary creatures trigger an additional time. Atla's trigger is one, so every Egg death reveals twice.
- [[Doubling Season]]: Atla makes two Eggs per activation. Also doubles the tokens from [[Hornet Queen]] and [[Palani's Hatcher]].
- [[Seedborn Muse]] (Game Changer): untaps your permanents in each other player's untap step, so Atla makes Eggs on every turn, and so does your sacrifice engine.
- [[Natural Order]] (Game Changer): sacrifice a green creature (an Egg is green) to put any green creature from your library onto the battlefield. The Egg dying also triggers Atla.
- [[Rhythm of the Wild]]: your nontoken creatures get riot, so a hatched fatty can get haste or a counter, and nothing you cast can be countered.
- [[Selvala, Heart of the Wilds]]: whenever another creature enters, its controller may draw a card if its power is greater than each other creature's power. Your jackpots (and the table's) pay out in cards.

## Tips
- One sacrifice outlet and a few Eggs is already a turn. Don't wait for a second outlet before you start rolling.
- Mulligan for mana: a hand of fatties and no walls or rocks does nothing. Keep hands with a wall, a signet or Sol Ring.
- Keep [[Blasphemous Act]] and [[Heroic Intervention]] for the right moment. The Act cheaply kills a wide table, including your own Eggs, and each Egg is a reveal.
- Don't sweat bad flips. A revealed Wall of Roots just means you ramped, and the rest go to the bottom.
