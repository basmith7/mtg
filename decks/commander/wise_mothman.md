# The Wise Mothman – Glow Up

A Sultai Fallout deck that irradiates the table. Everyone mills, and every nonland card that hits a graveyard makes your creatures bigger.

## What rad counters do
Rad counters sit on players, not permanents. At the start of each player's precombat main phase, a player with rad counters mills that many cards. For each nonland card milled, they lose 1 life and remove one rad counter. Milled lands don't remove counters. Proliferate adds rad counters as well as +1/+1 counters.

## How it plays
- **Early:** Ramp (Farseek, Three Visits, signets) and cheap millers like [[Hedron Crab]] and [[Stitcher's Supplier]]. Cast Mothman on turn 4 (about 92% of games; turn 3 only about half the time, and only with ramp). Its enter trigger gives everyone a rad counter, so each player's next main phase grows your board.
- **Mid:** Spread rad counters with [[Nightkin Ambusher]], [[Mirelurk Queen]] and [[Vault 12: The Necropolis]], then proliferate them with [[Contentious Plan]], [[Evolution Sage]] and [[Flux Channeler]]. Attack with Mothman for more rads. Every mill triggers Mothman again.
- **Late:** [[Bruvac the Grandiloquent]] doubles your opponents' rad mill. Bring back your best cards with [[Living Death]], [[Six]] and [[The Master, Transcendent]].

## How it wins
- **Fliers with counters:** Mothman and [[Screeching Scorchbeast]] get very big very fast.
- **Mill:** rad counters plus Bruvac, [[Fraying Sanity]] and [[Phenax, God of Deception]]. Once your creatures are huge, Phenax lets each one mill a player for its toughness.
- No infinite or game-winning combos in the deck (the combo checker lists none). Phenax with big creatures is the strongest finisher, but it needs a built-up board.

## Key cards
- [[The Wise Mothman]]: each player gets a rad counter when it enters or attacks. Whenever nonland cards are milled, put a +1/+1 counter on each of up to X target creatures, where X is how many were milled.
- [[Bruvac the Grandiloquent]]: if an opponent would mill cards, they mill twice that many instead. That includes rad mill.
- [[Mirelurk Queen]]: gives a player two rad counters when it enters. Once a turn, when nonland cards are milled, you draw a card and it gets a +1/+1 counter.
- [[Hardened Scales]]: each time +1/+1 counters would go on one of your creatures, it gets one extra. [[Corpsejack Menace]] is the doubler, and [[Winding Constrictor]] adds one to counters on your creatures and on you, rad counters included.
- [[Phenax, God of Deception]]: your creatures gain "{T}: Target player mills X cards," where X is that creature's toughness.
- [[Nuclear Fallout]]: each creature gets twice -X/-X (that is -2X/-2X), and each player gets X rad counters. Your pumped-up creatures survive it.
- [[Struggle for Project Purity]]: Brotherhood: at your upkeep each opponent draws a card, and you draw a card for each card drawn that way. Enclave makes anyone who attacks you take two rad counters per attacker.

## Tips
- You get rad counters too. Your own mill fuels Mothman and the graveyard, but watch your life total. Glowing One and [[Mariposa Military Base]] help, and [[Winding Constrictor]] adds to your own rad counters too.
- [[Heroic Intervention]] is your protection: hold {1}{G} open when you cast [[Damnation]] so your counter-stacked board survives. It doesn't save anything from [[Living Death]], which makes players sacrifice. [[Nuclear Fallout]] is safe on its own, since your pumped creatures live.
- Proliferate is your choice. Skip the player who's losing and pile rads on the leader.
- Mulligan for 2+ lands, a ramp piece, and either a turn-4 Mothman or an early rad source.
