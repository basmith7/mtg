Xantcha starts on an opponent's side of the table and has to attack every turn, but never you. The rest of the deck does the same thing at scale: cursed gifts, goad and forced combat, until the table has beaten itself up and you drain whoever is left.

## How it plays
- **Early:** rocks ([[Sol Ring]], Signets, Talismans) and cheap chaos: [[Sleeper Agent]], [[Bloodthirsty Blade]], [[Stormfist Crusader]] and a curse ([[Curse of Opulence]], [[Curse of Disturbance]]) on the player everyone wants to hit.
- **Mid:** cast Xantcha on turn 3 (about 96% of games) and hand her to whoever will make the most trouble with a 5/5 that must attack someone other than you. Then spread goad: the Impetus auras, [[Agitator Ant]], [[Vengeful Ancestor]], [[Geode Rager]], [[Karazikar, the Eye Tyrant]], and [[Fumiko the Lowblood]], which makes every opponent's creature attack each combat.
- **Late:** [[Disrupt Decorum]] or an overloaded [[Spectacular Showdown]] for one huge swing, then finish with drain.

## How it wins
- **The table kills itself:** goaded creatures, [[Death Kiss]] doubling their power, and [[Sower of Discord]] linking two players' life totals.
- **Drain:** [[Exsanguinate]], [[Choice of Damnations]] or [[Prisoner's Dilemma]] on a weakened table.
- **Damage redirect:** [[Brash Taunter]] or [[Stuffy Doll]] with [[Blasphemous Act]] deals 13 to a player.
- The combo checker finds no included combos, so there is no combo finish.

## Key cards
- [[Xantcha, Sleeper Agent]]: enters under an opponent's control, attacks each combat if able and can't attack you. Any player may pay {3} to make Xantcha's controller lose 2 life, and whoever pays draws a card.
- [[Heartstone]]: creatures' activated abilities cost {1} less, so Xantcha's costs {2}. It works for everyone, so opponents get the discount too.
- [[Fumiko the Lowblood]]: creatures your opponents control attack each combat if able.
- [[Stormfist Crusader]]: at your upkeep each player draws a card and loses 1 life.
- [[Black Market Connections]]: each main phase, choose any of a Treasure (lose 1), a card (lose 2) or a 3/2 changeling (lose 3).
- [[Assault Suit]]: the equipped creature can't attack you or be sacrificed, and at each opponent's upkeep you may lend it to them, untapped.
- [[Captive Audience]]: enters under an opponent's control; each of their upkeeps they pick a new mode: life becomes 4, discard their hand, or every other player gets five 2/2 Zombies.
- [[Sudden Spoiling]]: split second; a player's creatures lose all abilities and become 0/2 until end of turn. Your answer to a lethal swing.

## Tips
- Give Xantcha to the player most likely to attack the leader, not the one with the weakest board.
- Pay for Xantcha's ability at the end of turns when you have spare mana: it draws you cards and drains her holder.
- Your life total is a resource but not infinite: Black Market Connections, Stormfist and the Talisman all cost life. [[No Mercy]] and [[Crawlspace]] keep attackers off you.
- Mulligan for 2+ lands and a rock. Xantcha is cheap, so the hand needs follow-up chaos, not ramp.
