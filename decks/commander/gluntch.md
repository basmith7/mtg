# Gluntch – Trojan Horse Bearing Gifts

Gluntch hands out counters, cards and Treasure every turn, and everyone at the table likes you. The deck is built so that every gift comes back to you with interest. By the time anyone notices, you're at 50 life with a board full of counters.

## How it plays
- **Early:** ramp ([[Nature's Lore]], [[Three Visits]], mana dorks, Signets) and set up the "tax" pieces: [[Smothering Tithe]], [[Monologue Tax]], [[Esper Sentinel]], [[Archivist of Oghma]], [[Smuggler's Share]]. Cast [[Ghostly Prison]] or [[Windborn Muse]] so attacking you costs more than leaving you alone.
- **Mid:** land Gluntch (a 0/5 flyer that's easy to keep alive). Give the counters to yourself or to whoever is fighting the leader, the card draw to an opponent who has Tithe and Archivist on the table, and the Treasure to yourself when [[Academy Manufactor]] is out. Group ramp like [[Collective Voyage]], [[Tempt with Discovery]] and [[Veteran Explorer]] makes friends and also triggers Archivist and Smuggler's Share.
- **Late:** counter doublers ([[Doubling Season]], [[Hardened Scales]], [[Branching Evolution]]) and [[Cathars' Crusade]] turn your own share of the gifts into a real army.

## How it wins
- **Life total:** Archivist triggers, Food from Manufactor and pillowfort cards that slow attackers down let you win with [[Felidar Sovereign]] at 40 or [[Test of Endurance]] at 50. [[Aetherflux Reservoir]] is the backup plan.
- **Overrun:** [[Craterhoof Behemoth]] (find it with [[Finale of Devastation]]), [[Gavony Township]] and [[Akroma's Will]] on a board grown by counters.
- **Big flyer:** Gluntch putting two counters on itself every turn, doubled, becomes a real threat.
- No infinite combos.

## Key cards
- [[Gluntch, the Bestower]]: at your end step, one player puts two +1/+1 counters on a creature they control, a second player draws a card, and a third creates two Treasure tokens.
- [[Smothering Tithe]]: whenever an opponent draws a card, they may pay {2}; if they don't, you create a Treasure. Pair it with Gluntch's draw gift.
- [[Academy Manufactor]]: if you would create a Clue, Food or Treasure token, you create one of each instead. Food gains life for the life-total win.
- [[Archivist of Oghma]]: whenever an opponent searches their library, you gain 1 life and draw a card. Your group ramp spells set it off.
- [[Smuggler's Share]]: at each end step, draw for each opponent who drew two or more cards that turn, and make a Treasure for each opponent who had two or more lands enter.
- [[Monologue Tax]]: whenever an opponent casts their second spell each turn, you create a Treasure. The mana you give away turns into Treasure for you.
- [[Tempt with Glory]]: you put a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control. Each opponent may do the same for their creatures, and for each one who does, you get another round.
- [[Generous Gift]]: destroy target permanent; its controller gets a 3/3 Elephant. Removal that's also a present.
- [[Felidar Sovereign]] / [[Test of Endurance]]: win at your upkeep with 40 / 50 or more life.

## Tips
- Spread the gifts out loudly. Give counters to whoever is losing, so the leader stays busy.
- Give the draw to someone who won't pay {2} for Smothering Tithe.
- Hold [[Teferi's Protection]] or [[Heroic Intervention]] for when someone finally works out what you're doing.
- [[Farewell]] and [[Austere Command]] let you choose which modes to use, so you can keep your enchantments.
- Mulligan for two or more lands plus ramp, or a tax piece. Gluntch doesn't need to come down early.
