# Gluntch – Trojan Horse Bearing Gifts

Gluntch hands out counters, cards and Treasure every turn, and everyone at the table likes you. The deck is built so that every gift comes back to you with interest. By the time anyone notices, you have a board full of counters and a [[Craterhoof Behemoth]] in hand.

## How it plays
- **Early:** ramp ([[Nature's Lore]], [[Three Visits]], mana dorks, Signets) and set up the "tax" pieces: [[Smothering Tithe]], [[Monologue Tax]], [[Esper Sentinel]], [[Archivist of Oghma]], [[Smuggler's Share]]. [[Flumph]] and [[Master of Ceremonies]] add cheap gift bodies. Cast [[Ghostly Prison]] or [[Windborn Muse]] so attacking you costs more than leaving you alone. [[Tenuous Truce]] (you and the enchanted opponent each draw at their end step) fuels Tithe and Sentinel, and falls off if either of you attacks the other.
- **Mid:** land Gluntch (a 0/5 flyer that's easy to keep alive). Give the counters to yourself or whoever is fighting the leader, and the Treasure to yourself when [[Academy Manufactor]] is out. Group ramp like [[Collective Voyage]], [[Tempt with Discovery]] and [[Veteran Explorer]] makes friends and also triggers Archivist and Smuggler's Share.
- **Late:** counter doublers ([[Doubling Season]], [[Hardened Scales]], [[Branching Evolution]]) and [[Cathars' Crusade]] turn your own share of the gifts into a real army.

## How it wins
- **Overrun (main plan):** [[Craterhoof Behemoth]] (find it with [[Finale of Devastation]]), [[Gavony Township]] and [[Akroma's Will]] on a board of about 20 creatures grown by counters.
- **Life total (long shot):** [[Felidar Sovereign]] wins at 40 life, but lifegain is thin (Archivist, Food from Manufactor, Felidar's lifelink), so don't count on it. [[Aetherflux Reservoir]] is a backup.
- **Big flyer:** Gluntch putting two counters on itself every turn, doubled, becomes a real threat.
- No infinite combos.

## Key cards
- [[Gluntch, the Bestower]]: at your end step, one player puts two +1/+1 counters on a creature they control, a second player draws a card, and a third creates two Treasure tokens.
- [[Smothering Tithe]]: whenever an opponent draws a card, they may pay {2}; if they don't, you create a Treasure. Pair it with Gluntch's draw gift.
- [[Academy Manufactor]]: if you would create a Clue, Food or Treasure token, you create one of each instead. Food gains life for the life-total win.
- [[Archivist of Oghma]]: whenever an opponent searches their library, you gain 1 life and draw a card. [[Veteran Explorer]] and [[Collective Voyage]] are the main triggers.
- [[Esper Sentinel]]: a creature; whenever an opponent casts their first noncreature spell each turn, you draw unless they pay {X}, where X is its power (usually 1).
- [[Tempt with Glory]]: you put a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control; each opponent may do the same, and you repeat for each who does.
- [[Generous Gift]]: destroy target permanent; its controller gets a 3/3 Elephant. Removal that's also a present.
- [[Master of Ceremonies]]: at your upkeep each opponent picks money, friends or secrets, and you and that player both get the matching Treasure, 1/1 Citizen or card.
- [[Felidar Sovereign]]: at your upkeep, if you have 40 or more life, you win. Hard to reach here.

## Tips
- Spread the gifts out loudly. Give counters to whoever is losing, so the leader stays busy.
- Give the draw to someone who won't pay {2} for Smothering Tithe.
- Hold [[Teferi's Protection]] or [[Heroic Intervention]] for when someone finally works out what you're doing.
- [[Farewell]] and [[Austere Command]] let you choose which modes to use, so you can keep your enchantments.
- Mulligan for two or more lands plus ramp, or a tax piece. Gluntch doesn't need to come down early.
