Ruric Thar hits anyone who casts a noncreature spell for 6, so the whole table gets pushed into playing creatures, and nobody plays bigger creatures than this deck. Ramp hard, drop the biggest creatures you can, and make every removal spell your opponents cast hurt.

Built on willy's (@unmagical.mtg) "54 Feral Hogs" list ([Moxfield](https://moxfield.com/decks/t9G16DLc5EO0uYOuDftBIQ)), cut down to fewer noncreature spells and topped with more big creatures.

## How it plays
- **Early:** mana creatures. [[Llanowar Elves]], [[Birds of Paradise]], [[Paradise Druid]], [[Incubation Druid]], [[Wood Elves]] and [[Sakura-Tribe Elder]] get Ruric out early, usually by turn 5. Only ten cards in the deck aren't creatures or lands, so Ruric rarely hurts you. Cast [[Sol Ring]] and [[Rhythm of the Wild]] before Ruric lands.
- **Mid:** Ruric comes down and has to attack every combat. Vigilance keeps it home as a 6/6 reach blocker too. Back it up with engines: [[Beast Whisperer]] and [[Soul of the Harvest]] draw cards, [[Goreclaw, Terror of Qal Sisma]] cuts the cost of big creatures, and [[Garruk's Horde]] lets you cast creatures off the top of your library.
- **Late:** big threats that are good the turn they land: [[Terror of the Peaks]], [[Inferno Titan]], [[Ghalta, Primal Hunger]], [[Elder Gargaroth]], [[Molten Primordial]], then a finisher.

## How it wins
- **Overrun:** [[Craterhoof Behemoth]], [[Pathbreaker Ibex]] or [[End-Raze Forerunners]] on a wide board. [[Natural Order]], [[Survival of the Fittest]], [[Fierce Empath]] and [[Yisan, the Wanderer Bard]] find them.
- **Commander damage:** Ruric attacks every turn anyway. [[Garruk's Uprising]] gives it trample, and [[Kessig Wolf Run]] or [[Rogue's Passage]] push the damage through.
- **Punishment:** Ruric and [[Scytheclaw Raptor]] steadily drain players who keep casting spells.
- **Primal Surge:** 94 of the 100 cards are permanents, so [[Primal Surge]] usually dumps most of your library onto the table.
- No infinite combos.

## Key cards
- [[Ruric Thar, the Unbowed]]: 6/6 with reach and vigilance. It attacks each combat if able. Whenever a player casts a noncreature spell, it deals 6 damage to that player, and that includes you.
- [[Terror of the Peaks]]: whenever another creature you control enters, it deals damage equal to that creature's power to any target. Spells your opponents cast that target it cost 3 extra life.
- [[Ilharg, the Raze-Boar]]: when it attacks, put a creature from your hand onto the battlefield tapped and attacking. It goes back to your hand at the end step, and you can drop it again next turn. Pairs well with Terror.
- [[Gruul Ragebeast]]: whenever it or another creature you control enters, that creature fights target creature an opponent controls. Your big creatures double as removal, but the fight isn't optional: a mana dork that enters while an opponent has a creature has to fight it. Cast Ragebeast after your dorks are down.
- [[Scytheclaw Raptor]]: whenever a player casts a spell when it isn't their turn, it deals 4 damage to them. With Ruric on the table, a removal spell cast on someone else's turn costs that player 10 life (6 on their own turn).
- [[Raggadragga, Goreguts Boss]]: your mana creatures get +2/+2 and untap when they attack. Whenever you cast a spell with seven or more mana spent on it, untap target creature and give it +7/+7 and trample.
- [[Garruk's Horde]]: play with the top card of your library revealed, and cast creature spells from there.
- [[Craterhoof Behemoth]]: haste. When it enters, your creatures gain trample and get +X/+X, where X is the number of creatures you control.
- [[Rhythm of the Wild]]: creature spells you control can't be countered, and your nontoken creatures have riot (each enters with your choice of a +1/+1 counter or haste).
- [[Gaea's Cradle]]: tap for {G} for each creature you control.

## Tips
- Your noncreature spells hit you too. Cast [[Natural Order]], [[Chaos Warp]] or [[Beast Within]] only when 6 life is worth it, and keep [[Heroic Intervention]] for a board wipe.
- [[Bane of Progress]] destroys all artifacts and enchantments, yours included (Sol Ring, Rhythm, Survival, Garruk's Uprising). Cast it when the table's rocks and enchantments matter more than yours.
- Ruric must attack. Point it at whoever relies most on noncreature spells. They're the ones Ruric slows down the most.
- [[Etali, Primal Storm]] can cast noncreature spells off the top of opponents' libraries, and Ruric hits you for each one. You choose what to cast, so skip them when your life is low.
- [[Spinerock Knoll]] needs an opponent to have taken 7 damage that turn, and one Ruric hit plus any attack gets you there. [[Mosswort Bridge]] needs total power 10, which is usually true.
- Mulligan for two or more lands plus a mana creature. Ruric is your best card, so get it out on time.
