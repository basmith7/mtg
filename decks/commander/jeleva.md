Jeleva is a flying Vampire Wizard who loots every library at the table, then lets you cast the good stuff for free. Everyone's best spells (including yours) end up in a pile, and you pick the winners. It's the Xantcha energy Brian liked: the table's cards do the work.

## How it plays
- **Early:** ramp (Sol Ring, three Talismans, three Signets) into a turn-3 or turn-4 [[Jeleva, Nephalia's Scourge]]. Counterspells and cheap cantrips keep you honest while you dig.
- **Mid:** Jeleva's enter trigger exiles the top X cards of every library, where X is the mana spent on her. Each attack, you may cast one exiled instant or sorcery free. Protect her with [[Lightning Greaves]] and [[Swiftfoot Boots]], then flicker her with [[Ghostly Flicker]] or bounce her with [[Riptide Laboratory]] for a fresh pile.
- **Late:** big mana (Treasure from [[Storm-Kiln Artist]], [[Prosper, Tome-Bound]], [[Hit the Mother Lode]]) powers table-wide haymakers.

## How it wins
- **Combat plus free spells:** Jeleva flies, and every attack is a free spell from somebody's deck. [[Thief of Sanity]] and [[Etali, Primal Storm]] do the same tricks.
- **Burn and drain:** [[Comet Storm]], [[Crackle with Power]], [[Torment of Hailfire]] and [[Guttersnipe]] close once the mana is there.
- **Theft:** [[Insurrection]] hits for everyone's creatures at once; [[Bribery]] takes the best creature from an opponent's library.
- No infinite combos are in the deck (the checker lists 0 included combos). Combos that are one card away exist, but none are built in.

## Key cards
- [[Jeleva, Nephalia's Scourge]]: ETB exiles the top X of each library; on attack, cast one exiled instant or sorcery free.
- [[Plargg and Nassari]]: each upkeep, every player exiles until a nonland card; an opponent picks one, and you cast up to two of the rest free.
- [[Mind's Dilation]]: an opponent's first spell each turn exiles their top card; if nonland, you may cast it free.
- [[Dream Harvest]]: each opponent exiles to total MV 5 or more, and you may cast those free this turn.
- [[Wild-Magic Sorcerer]]: your first spell cast from exile each turn has cascade, which chains nicely with Jeleva's free casts.
- [[Kess, Dissident Mage]]: once per turn, cast an instant or sorcery from your graveyard.
- [[Thousand-Year Storm]]: copies each spell for every instant or sorcery already cast this turn.
- [[Cyclonic Rift]]: overloaded for {6}{U}, it bounces every opposing nonland permanent. Counts as one of three Game Changers (with [[Rhystic Study]] and [[Jeska's Will]]).

## Tips
- Cast Jeleva with as much mana as you can. X is mana spent, so a 4-mana Jeleva sees four cards per library, and paying commander tax makes the pile bigger.
- Exiled cards stay with Jeleva, so a later attack can reach back to old piles.
- Opponents' removal is the main danger. Hold up [[Counterspell]] or put Greaves on her before swinging.
- Mulligan for a cheap rock plus a blue source; a hand with no way to reach four mana is a keep only with cantrips.
- Rhystic Study and the free-spell stacks make you a target. Tell the table what is in the pile; the memorable turns are the point.
